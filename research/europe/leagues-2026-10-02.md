## 1. "ULF" — what is it?

**Confidence: UNKNOWN that any European league called "ULF" exists. Most likely meaning: AFLE.**

- No European American-football league named "ULF" / "United League of Football" was found in 2025–2026.
- Plausible meanings of the request:
  1. **AFLE — American Football League Europe** (also styled "The League: Europe"), the *second* breakaway league from the ELF, alongside EFA. Its first season was played in 2026 (see section 1b). This is the most likely intended league.
  2. **UFL — United Football League**: a US spring league (not European). Its 2026 season ran March 27–May 31, playoffs June 7–13, with 8 teams. Not relevant to European rankings. (Search result: americanfootballinternational.com "United Football League 2026 Season: All You Need to Know"; not fetched in detail.)
  3. **ULAF** — Ukrainian League of American Football (national league only).
- **Recommendation:** ask the user to confirm; we should treat "ULF" as AFLE.

Source: elfdata.eu (European Leagues Football Data Lab). Wikipedia shows Munich 396 PF and Frankfurt 313 PA (1-point discrepancies). W-L confirmed by both. Confidence: CONFIRMED for W-L; REPORTED for PF/PA.

Playoffs:
- Semifinals (Frankfurt playoff weekend, 15–16 Aug 2026): Nordic Storm 45–6 Paris Musketeers; Munich Ravens 52–33 Frankfurt Galaxy (elfdata.eu; Wikipedia; football-world.news). Venue name differs between sources (Wikipedia "BBBank Arena"; Gridiron Magazine pre-season "PSD Bank Arena") — use "Frankfurt". CONFIRMED (scores).
- **Championship Game, 29 Aug 2026, Tivoli Stadion Tirol, Innsbruck (AUT): Nordic Storm 48–28 Munich Ravens.** Attendance 4,157 (Wikipedia). Game ended with a scuffle between players in the final minute (sn.dk). Danish WR Simon Føns scored two early TDs; QB Jadrian Clark scored the clinching TD (sn.dk). CONFIRMED.
- Season MVP: Wikipedia lists **Jadrian Clark (Nordic Storm, QB)**. football-world.news confirms only his nomination (3,726 pass yds, 47 TD). Winner announcement not found from an official source. REPORTED.
