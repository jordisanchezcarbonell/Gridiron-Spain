# Selección española — research dossier (2026-10-02)

Scope: every Spanish national team (tackle men/women, flag men/women, U19 tackle, U17/U15 flag).
Method: FEFA posts read via the WordPress REST API (`curl -k`, because of the fefa.es TLS issue); IFAF articles read via the americanfootball.sport REST API (`acf.content`); BAFA via WebFetch. Every row below cites a source I opened (see the Sources table at the end).

Confidence: **CONFIRMED** = official federation (FEFA/IFAF/BAFA). **REPORTED** = an official source, but with a gap or ambiguity (noted inline). **UNKNOWN** = not found, so don't publish.

## 1. Men's tackle team (Selección Absoluta Masculina)

### Conflict resolved: both October games are scheduled
| Claim | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Jan calendar: 17/18 Oct GB Dev vs Spain, 24/25 Oct Spain vs Georgia ("dates TBC") | FEFA 28 Jan 2026, BAFA 28 Jan 2026, FEFA 11 Feb 2026 | superseded |
| "España debutará en el Europeo el 24 de octubre en Jaca … ante Georgia" | FEFA 18 Sep 2026 (tryouts note) | contradicts IFAF |
| **Sun 18 Oct 2026 — GB Development Team vs Spain, Nick Newbold Stadium, Coventry, 12:00 local (13:00 CET)**<br>**Sat 24 Oct 2026 — Spain vs Georgia, Municipal Football Field, Jaca, 17:00 CET** | **IFAF, 29 Sep 2026** (newest official source) | **CONFIRMED** |

Conclusion: IFAF's 29 Sep schedule is the most recent official source, and it has Spain playing **away to GB Dev in Coventry first (18 Oct)**, then hosting Georgia in Jaca (24 Oct). The FEFA 18 Sep note doesn't mention the Coventry game. Its "debutará … 24 de octubre en Jaca" is most likely loose wording for the home debut, or it was written before the venue was set. Editorial recommendation: go with IFAF, but get FEFA to confirm before publishing, since FEFA has not yet posted its own Coventry announcement.

Other discrepancy: IFAF writes that "Spain returns for the first time since 2008" (29 Sep 2026), and AFI/IFAF said in Jan that Spain "last entered in 2018". FEFA records official Euro group-B games in 2022 (vs Ireland) and 2023 (vs Israel), shown below. **Do not repeat either "since 2008" or "since 2018".**
