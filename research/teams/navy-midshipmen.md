## Recent History & Context

### 2024-2025 Success
Navy achieved unprecedented success under Brian Newberry:
- School-record 21 combined wins over two seasons (10-3 in 2024, 11-2 in 2025)
- Back-to-back Commander-in-Chief's Trophy winners (first since 2012-13)
- Back-to-back bowl wins:
  - 2024: Armed Forces Bowl (W 21-20 vs Oklahoma)
  - 2025: Liberty Bowl (W 35-13 vs Cincinnati)
- Lambert Trophy winner in 2025 (best team in the East, first since 1963)

### Key Departures for 2026
- **Blake Horvath** (QB) - Career ended 2025
  - First Navy player to rush for 1,000+ yards since Malcolm Perry (2019)
  - First Navy QB to rush and pass for 1,000 yards in back-to-back seasons
  - Academic All-American, Division I Scholar-Athlete of the Year
  - Liberty Bowl MVP (2026), Armed Forces Bowl MVP (2024)
- **Landon Robinson** (NFL Draft)
- **Eli Heidenreich** (NFL Draft)

### Returning Experience
- 12 starters returning
- 46 letter winners
